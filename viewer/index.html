    How to navigate

    • Rotate: left-drag (one finger on touch screens)
    • Pan: right-drag or Shift + drag (two-finger drag on touch screens)
    • Zoom: mouse wheel (pinch on touch screens)
    • Select a building: click or tap it, or choose it from Buildings
    • Keyboard: ← / → previous/next building · Home = overview · L = labels · Esc = close panels
